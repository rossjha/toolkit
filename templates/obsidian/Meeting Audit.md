## 2. Rules of thumb

- Role **I** and value ≤ 2 → drop or go async (read the notes).
- Role **C** where someone in the team could represent → delegate (and treat it as a development opportunity).
- No linked priority and value ≤ 3 → challenge the cadence.
- Prep cost greater than meeting length → check whether the prep or the meeting is the real deliverable; consider moving the meeting earlier in the week to compress prep, or standardising the pre-read.
- Meetings you run (**R**) → default to 25/50 min, agenda in the invite, end early when done.
