# Obsidian templates

Templates for Obsidian's core Templates plugin (`{{title}}`, `{{date:YYYY-MM-DD}}`). Copy the ones you want into your vault's templates folder; nothing links them automatically.

| Template | Use |
|---|---|
| `Fortnightly Plan.md` | Two-week plan: wins, Top 5, time check, meeting prep. The `/tools:fortnightly-planning` skill keeps its own copy |
| `Meeting Audit.md` | Quarterly audit of standing and prep-heavy meetings |
| `Permanent Note.md` | Slipbox: one idea, titled as a claim, with reasoned links |
| `Literature Note.md` | One per source: own-words notes and ideas to promote to permanent notes |

Notes use a `type:` frontmatter field (`permanent`, `literature`) rather than tags, so Dataview can query them.
